I totally **love** Irish music, therefore i love this album and The Cranberries themselves. I consider this to be a totally *non-skip album*, with a specific mood (it feels like a garage on a particularly grey day in Dublin, after a long road-trip on the Irish coast) that settles between each track. I personally loved, besides ***Linger*** (which is, for me, an all time favorite)  *Waltzing Back*. 

This album accomplishes to merge traditional irish sounds and the 90's grunge-rock yearning, two things that i would consider a match made in heaven. I loved it a lot.
