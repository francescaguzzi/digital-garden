I re-listened today, on one of my long train journeys, to this incredible album. Of course, this contains many of The Kooks **best hits of all time**. 

I've loved this since i was 15 years old and even now, seven years later, this doesn't stop to amaze me. I love absolutely everything about this album, it is catchy, energetic, young, brilliant. 

I've also found myself enjoying more than usual the ending track, *Got No Love*, which i've pretty much ignored until now. 

I feel like *The Kooks always sound like they're next gen Skins main characters*.
