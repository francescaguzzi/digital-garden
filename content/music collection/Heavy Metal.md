The first debut album from **Geese**'s frontman, Cameron Winter, breaks out like something ageless: it's difficult to say, just by listening to it, in which year this album came out. 

Incredible to say, *just 2024*. This ageless folk piece is brilliant, deep in thoughts, and quite different from other contemporary folk albums seen around. 

There are a couple of very well written and composed tracks (*Nausicaa*, *Love Takes Miles*, *$0*) and i've also really appreciated the ending track - *Can't Keep Anything*. 

Overall, i'm not really fond of this. I don't know why: i love his voice, his style, the composing is amazing as well as the lyrics, but i kinda have the impression he's going out of timing (probably a desired effect)
