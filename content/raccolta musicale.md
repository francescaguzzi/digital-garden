I've always been a huge music listener, always on the move to find the next artist or track that would expand my taste. 

For this, in January 2024 I decided to listen to at least one album a day and collect my ratings and thoughts in a database. Of course, like any other thing I do, most of the time I didn't meet my expectations of listening to one entire album every day. But that's it! 🎶
